GETTING STARTED / 03

From download to Home Screen.

These apps are distributed through GitHub. Check your app’s requirements before installing.

  1. 01

    Choose your app

    Open its release page, read the compatibility notes, and download the IPA asset for that release.

  2. 02

    Sign & install

    Use your preferred compatible sideloading method. Its certificate and account rules determine how long the app remains signed.

  3. 03

    Make it yours

    Open the app and follow its setup. Coconut may need a PosterBoard UUID; Eagle checks device support before preparing access.

Already have Vendor? Sign an IPA on your iPhone

Import your own .p12 certificate and .mobileprovision profile into Vendor, enter the certificate password, then choose an IPA and tap Sign, followed by Install. Vendor itself must already be installed through another method. Expired or revoked certificates require re-signing with a valid certificate.
